How to Register Your Design Online in India

Protecting your creative work is essential in today's competitive market. What is Design Registration & Why It Matters

Design registration is a form of intellectual property protection that safeguards the visual appearance, shape, configuration, pattern, or ornament of a product. When you register your design online in India , you obtain exclusive rights to use that design for a specified period. The registration certificate serves as proof of ownership and provides legal protection against unauthorized copying or infringement.

Key commercial and operational advantages include:

  • Exclusive right to manufacture, sell, and distribute products with your design
  • Legal protection against unauthorized copying and infringement
  • Ability to recover damages from infringers in court proceedings
  • Enhanced product value and market credibility
  • Basis for filing international design applications under Hague Agreement
  • Licensing opportunities to generate additional revenue

Design registration in India is open to various applicants. You do not need to be a citizen of India to apply for design registration. Here are the categories of people who can apply:

Documents Required from You

To ensure a smooth filing process without department queries or delays, prepare the following documentation:

  • Completed Form 1 or Form 2 with all required information
  • 5 copies of clear design drawings (each showing different angles)
  • Statement of Novelty and Inventiveness highlighting unique features
  • Proof of ownership or authorization if filing on behalf of another
  • Identity proof of the applicant (PAN card, Aadhaar card, or Passport)
  • Address proof such as utility bills or rental agreement
  • Priority claim documents if claiming priority from another country
  • Power of attorney if filing through a representative or consultant

Ongoing Compliance & Next Steps

Design infringement occurs when someone manufactures, sells, or imports products using your registered design without permission. If you discover design infringement of your registered design , Indian courts provide several legal remedies and penalties for the infringing party.

Ensure continuous legal standing by maintaining the following post-registration requirements:

  • Initiate renewal 6 months before expiration of your current 10-year protection period
  • File renewal application (Form 4) with the Design Registry
  • Provide updated applicant information if there are changes
  • Pay applicable renewal fee (₹1,000 to ₹2,000 depending on applicant category)
  • Submit any required updated documents or declarations
  • Receive renewal approval within 2-3 weeks
