DIR 3 KYC - Application for KYC of Directors

A Director Identification Number (DIN) is a unique number assigned to an individual who wishes to become a director or is already serving as a director of a company. Earlier, obtaining a DIN was a one-time process that required submitting an application through e-Form DIR-3.

What is Dir 3 Kyc & Why It Matters

As mentioned, Form DIR-3 KYC is an electronic form mandated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) for the purpose of updating the KYC (Know Your Customer) details of individuals who have been allotted a Director Identification Number (DIN).

Key commercial and operational advantages include:

  • To be filed by a director who has already submitted DIR-3 KYC or DIR-3 KYC (Web) in a previous year, provided there is no change in KYC details.
  • In this version, the director’s basic information is pre-filled from the MCA database and cannot be edited.
  • Personal Contact Details: Provide your own mobile number and email address. These will be verified through a one-time password (OTP).
  • Digital Signature: The form must be signed using the director’s own digital signature .
  • Accuracy and Certification: Ensure that all details entered are complete and accurate. The form must be certified by a practising Chartered Accountant (CA), Company Secretary (CS), or Cost Accountant.

Who Needs This Service & Eligibility Criteria

Documents Required from You

To ensure a smooth filing process without department queries or delays, prepare the following documentation:

  • Permanent address proof – such as Voter ID, Driving Licence, or PAN card.
  • Present address proof – for example, a recent utility bill (not older than 2 months) or a rental agreement.
  • Aadhaar card.
  • Passport.
  • Other supporting documents (if applicable).
  • The Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) of the director (applicant) filing the form.
  • The DSC, membership number, and certificate of practice number of a practising professional, such as a Chartered Accountant (CA), Company Secretary (CS), or Cost Accountant.

Statutory Framework & Classifications

Directors must submit their KYC details each year, and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs offers two ways to do this. The type of form to be filed depends on whether it is the first submission or if any changes in details are required.

  • To be filed for the first time after a DIN is allotted.
  • Also required if there is any change or update in the director’s KYC details, such as a new mobile number or email address.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about DIN eKYC Filing Online in India.

The purpose of filing Form DIR-3 KYC is to conduct an annual Know Your Customer (KYC) process for directors by updating their personal details, contact information, and residential addresses with the Registrar of Companies (ROC). This form is essential for maintaining accurate and current director information in the MCA records.
Form DIR-3 KYC is a mandatory filing requirement for all individuals who have been allotted a Director Identification Number (DIN) and whose DIN status is 'approved'. This includes both existing directors and disqualified directors.
From the financial year 2019-20 onwards, every director who has been allotted a DIN on or before the end of a financial year must file Form DIR-3 KYC by 30th September of the immediately following financial year.
There are two types of DIR-3 KYC forms: DIR-3 KYC (Form) and DIR-3 KYC (Web). The former is filed for the first time or when there are changes in the director's KYC details, while the latter is filed annually if there are no changes in the director's KYC details.
If a director fails to file Form DIR-3 KYC by 30th September of the relevant year, their Director Identification Number (DIN) will be marked as "Deactivated due to Non-filing of DIR-3 KYC" by the MCA. To reactivate the DIN, the director must file the pending DIR-3 KYC and pay a late fee of ₹5,000.
The documents required for filing DIR-3 KYC include permanent address proof, present address proof, Aadhaar card, passport, and other supporting documents (if applicable). Additionally, the director must have a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) and the DSC, membership number, and certificate of practice number of a practising professional (CA, CS, or Cost Accountant).
To file DIR-3 KYC form online, you need to log in to the MCA website, navigate to the 'Company e-Filing' section, and select 'DIN Related Filings' followed by 'Form DIR-3 KYC' or 'Form DIR-3 KYC Web'. Then, follow the step-by-step process of entering personal and identification details, uploading attachments, and digitally signing the form.
Upon successful submission of the DIR-3 KYC form, a Service Request Number (SRN) is generated automatically. This SRN acts as a unique reference and should be carefully saved for any future correspondence or communication with the MCA.
After filing the DIR-3 KYC form, an official email confirming the approval and receipt of the form will be sent to the personal email ID of the applicant (director). This confirmation email serves as proof that the filing process has been successfully completed.
