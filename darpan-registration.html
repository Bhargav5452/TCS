NGO Darpan Registration is a mandatory process for all Non-Governmental Organizations and Voluntary Organizations seeking government grants, FCRA approval, or CSR funding in India. What is NGO Darpan Registration Online for India & Why It Matters

Darpan Registration is an online initiative by the Government of India under NITI Aayog that provides a unique identification number — the Darpan Unique ID — to all registered Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and Voluntary Organizations (VOs). The term "DARPAN" stands for Database and Profiling of Voluntary and Non-profit Organizations . It serves as a centralized platform that bridges the gap between civil society organizations and the government, ensuring that government grants for NGOs reach the right hands.

Key commercial and operational advantages include:

  • Darpan Registration is not merely a formality. It is a critical compliance requirement for all NGOs and VOs operating in India. Here is why every organization must prioritize it:

  • Complete transparency in fund utilization across all registered entities
  • Accountability of nonprofit organisations India at the national level
