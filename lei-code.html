Legal Entity Identifier Number Registration Online in India

A Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number is a unique 20-character alphanumeric code assigned to legal entities participating in financial transactions. Businesses, banks, and financial institutions in India must obtain an LEI registration to comply with RBI mandates and global financial regulations. For seamless business registrations and compliance services in India, Tirumala Consultancy Services offers end-to-end support to help entities obtain their LEI number quickly and accurately.

What is Legal Entity Identifier Number Registration & Why It Matters

A Legal Entity Identifier number is a globally recognized unique identification code issued to legal entities involved in financial transactions. Introduced by the Financial Stability Board (FSB) and endorsed by the G20, the LEI system was created to improve transparency in global financial markets. In India, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has made LEI registration mandatory for entities participating in large-value financial transactions, over-the-counter derivatives, and foreign currency transactions.

Key commercial and operational advantages include:

  • Global Recognition: An LEI number provides a universally accepted identity for your business in international financial markets
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensures compliance with RBI, SEBI, and international regulatory requirements
  • Improved Creditworthiness: Enhances trust and credibility with financial institutions and counterparties
  • Faster Transaction Processing: Reduces delays in large-value financial transactions
  • Risk Management: Helps in better assessment and management of counterparty risks
  • Access to Global Markets: Enables participation in international financial markets and cross-border transactions

Who Needs This Service & Eligibility Criteria

The following entities are required to complete LEI registration online as per RBI and SEBI guidelines:

Applicability and qualifying requirements include:

  • All companies registered under the Companies Act 2013
  • Banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)
  • Insurance companies and mutual funds
  • Partnership firms and Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs)
  • Trusts, societies, and other legal entities involved in financial transactions
  • Foreign entities participating in Indian financial markets

Documents Required from You

To ensure a smooth filing process without department queries or delays, prepare the following documentation:

  • Valid government-issued identity proof of the authorized signatory
  • Address proof of the registered office of the entity
  • Latest audited financial statements of the entity
  • Board resolution authorizing the LEI registration application
  • Any other documents as specified by the LEI issuing authority

Statutory Framework & Classifications

An LEI code is a 20-character alphanumeric identifier structured as follows:

  • Characters 1-4: Prefix identifying the LEI issuing organization (Local Operating Unit)
  • Characters 5-18: Unique entity-specific identifier assigned to the legal entity
  • Characters 19-20: Two check digits used for validation and error detection
  • Ultimate holding company information if the entity is a subsidiary
  • Direct parent entity details including name and registration number
  • Business registration number such as CIN, LLPIN, or registration certificate number
