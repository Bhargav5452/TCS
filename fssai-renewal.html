FSSAI Registration Certificate Renewal Online India

Every food business operator in India holding a valid FSSAI registration certificate must renew it before the expiry date to continue operating legally. The FSSAI license renewal process is governed by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India under the FSS Act, 2006. Whether you hold a basic registration, state license, or central license, timely FSSAI renewal online ensures uninterrupted business operations and full food safety compliance India.

What is FSSAI Registration Certificate Renewal Online India & Why It Matters

FSSAI registration certificate renewal is the process of extending the validity of your existing food license or basic registration before it lapses. All food businesses — from small home-based operators to large manufacturers — are required to renew their certificates periodically to remain compliant with India's food safety regulations.

Key commercial and operational advantages include:

  • Basic registration is valid for 1 to 5 years as chosen by the applicant
  • State and central licenses carry a validity of 1 to 5 years based on fee paid
  • Renewal applications must be submitted before the expiry date on the FoSCoS portal
  • A late renewal attracts a penalty of Rs. 100 per day beyond the expiry date

Who Needs This Service & Eligibility Criteria

Every food business operator holding any category of FSSAI license or registration is required to complete the renewal process before expiry. Understanding which category applies to your business is critical to ensuring the correct renewal form and fee are submitted through the FSSAI FoSCoS portal renewal .

Documents Required from You

To ensure a smooth filing process without department queries or delays, prepare the following documentation:

  • Existing FSSAI license or registration certificate copy
  • Photo ID and address proof of the proprietor or authorized signatory
  • Proof of business address (rental agreement or ownership document)
  • List of food products to be manufactured or handled
  • Form B duly completed and signed by the authorized person
  • NOC from the municipality or local body (if applicable)
  • Food safety management plan or certificate (for central license renewals)
  • Annual turnover declaration for determining the appropriate license category
